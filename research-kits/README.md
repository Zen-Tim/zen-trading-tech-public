# Research Kits

Portable studies you can run yourself. Each kit holds the data or the steps to export it, the rules, a prompt for an AI chat, and a reference report to check your result against.

| # | Kit | Question | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Daily Range Distribution](./Daily-Range-Distribution) | How big is a normal day, measured against the recent average range? | FDAX daily |
| 2 | [FDAX Pre-Market Breakout](./FDAX-Pre-Market-Breakout) | How often does Xetra break the overnight and pre-open ranges, and in which direction? | FDAX 15-minute |
| 3 | [Consecutive Bar Streaks](./Consecutive-Bar-Streaks) | After N bull or bear bars in a row, how often does the next bar continue? | ES weekly |
| 4 | [Weekly Gap Close](./Weekly-Gap-Close) | How often does price trade back to last week's close, on which day, and how many weeks does it take? | ES weekly from daily RTH |
| 5 | [Bar 1 High or Low of the Day](./Bar-1-High-Or-Low-Of-Day) | How often is the first 5-minute bar the high or the low of the day? | ES and FDAX 5-minute |
| 6 | [First 90 Minutes High or Low](./First-90-Minutes-High-Or-Low) | How often is the high or the low of the day in by the end of bar 18? | ES and FDAX 5-minute |
| 7 | [ES Pre-Market Breakout](./ES-Pre-Market-Breakout) | How often does the ES day session break the Globex high or low? | ES 15-minute, 24-hour |
| 8 | [20 EMA Daily Touch](./20-EMA-Daily-Touch) | How often does price touch the 20 EMA on the 5-minute chart during the day? | ES and FDAX 5-minute |
