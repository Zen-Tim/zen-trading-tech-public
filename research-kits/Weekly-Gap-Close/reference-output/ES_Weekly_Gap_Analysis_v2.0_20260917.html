Trading implications: Mark last week's RTH close on the chart every Monday. It is the level price is most likely to trade back to this week. Gap size tells you how likely that is, and how soon. This study shows where price tends to go. How to trade it is up to your own style.

Method: Week open = the first trading day's open. Week close = the last trading day's close. Weekly gap = this week's open minus last week's close. Gap size = the gap as a % of weekly ABR, the average high-to-low range of the previous 8 weekly bars. A gap is closed when any later day's high and low straddle last week's close. The study starts at the first week with 8 weeks of range behind it. Two storm-closure days (29 and 30 Oct 2012) are left out. "First trading day" is Monday, or Tuesday when Monday is a holiday.
