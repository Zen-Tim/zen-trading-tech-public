Bull runs lean continuation
At every reliable sample depth (2–8 consecutive bull bars), the next week is more likely to be bull than bear. The unconditional bull rate of 56.1% acts as a floor; 4 consecutive bull bars produces the strongest reading at 67.2%.
Bear runs lean reversal
At every reliable sample depth (2–4 consecutive bear bars), the next week is more likely to reverse to bull. Continuation probability stays below 47% across all three reliable rows. Bear is the more mean-reverting side of this market.
